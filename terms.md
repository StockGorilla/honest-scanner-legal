## 1. What Honest Scanner is

Honest Scanner turns paper into PDFs on your iPhone. Every step —
capture, filter, OCR, PDF assembly, password protection, signature
flatten, merge — happens on your device. There is no account, no cloud
storage, no analytics, and no tracking.

## 2. Free vs. Pro

- **Free**: unlimited scans, multi-page PDF export, filters, folders,
  local search over titles, share PDF, save to Files.
- **Pro** (optional purchase): searchable PDFs (on-device OCR),
  signature, password protection, merge documents. Pro is billed by
  Apple through the App Store as a one-time non-consumable

  trial. No weekly subscription.
