# Support

Need help with Honest Scanner?

- **Email:** [support@honestscanner.com](mailto:support@honestscanner.com)
- **GitHub Issues:** [github.com/StockGorilla/honest-scanner-legal/issues](https://github.com/StockGorilla/honest-scanner-legal/issues)

We usually reply to email within 1–2 business days.

## Common questions

**How do I restore my Pro purchase on a new device?**
Open **Settings** in the app → **Restore purchase**. Make sure you're
signed into the same Apple ID that made the original purchase. Pro
Lifetime supports Family Sharing.

**I forgot my document password.**
Password-protected PDFs cannot be recovered without the password. We
never store, transmit, or receive your document passwords, so we cannot
help decrypt a locked file. The original unlocked PDF was preserved in
the app when you locked a copy — check the doc detail page for it.

**Where are my scans stored?**
On your iPhone only. Nothing is uploaded to our servers. Uninstalling
the app deletes every scan.

**Can I use this on iPad?**
Yes. The app is universal and works on iPhone and iPad.

**Do you have an Android version?**
Not yet. iOS is our sole focus for v1.

**A feature broke after an update.**
Force-quit the app and reopen it. If the issue persists, email us with
the app version (Settings → Version) and a short description.
