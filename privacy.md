# Privacy Policy

**Effective 2026-08-27**

## Your scans never leave this phone

Every capture, filter, OCR pass, and PDF write happens on-device using
Apple's Vision and PDFKit frameworks. Nothing is uploaded to Honest
Scanner or any third party. The only time a file leaves the device is
when you tap **Share** — and it goes wherever you send it.

## No account

There is no sign-up, no email, no phone number, and no anonymous
identifier we assign you. Delete the app and every trace is gone.

## Purchases go through Apple

Pro is billed by the App Store. We receive an entitlement flag from
StoreKit. We never see your card, name, or Apple ID.

## No tracking SDKs

- No analytics that upload page contents.
- No crash reporter that attaches your documents.
- No advertising identifiers.
- No third-party telemetry of any kind.

## Permissions we ask for

- **Camera** — to scan documents.
- **Photo library** — only when the built-in document camera is
  unavailable on your device (for example on iOS simulators during
  development), so you can pick an image to import instead.
